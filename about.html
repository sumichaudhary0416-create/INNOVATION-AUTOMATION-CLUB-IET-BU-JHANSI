WHO WE ARE

Innovation & Automation Club

IAC is a student-driven technical platform focused on practical learning, innovation, automation, IoT, embedded systems and real-world project development.

The club encourages experimentation, prototyping, teamwork and implementation of engineering concepts through hands-on activities.
