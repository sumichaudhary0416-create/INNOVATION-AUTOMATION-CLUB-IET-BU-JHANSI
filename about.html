INNOVATION & AUTOMATION CLUB

Building Ideas Into Impact

The Innovation & Automation Club is a student-driven technical platform of the Electronics & Instrumentation Engineering Department. IAC encourages practical learning, innovation, automation, project development and real-world problem solving.

Our Vision

To create an innovative technical ecosystem where students learn, experiment, collaborate and transform ideas into practical solutions.

Our Mission

To bridge academic learning and industrial applications through hands-on training, projects and collaboration.
