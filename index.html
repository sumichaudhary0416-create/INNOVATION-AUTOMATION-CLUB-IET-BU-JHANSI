About IAC

The Innovation & Automation Club (IAC) is a student-driven platform that encourages innovation, creativity, and practical learning. It brings together students who are curious about technology, problem-solving, and building ideas into meaningful solutions. IAC provides opportunities to learn beyond the classroom through workshops, technical sessions, hands-on projects, competitions, and collaborative activities. The club focuses on bridging the gap between theoretical knowledge and real-world application while helping students develop technical as well as essential skills such as teamwork, leadership, communication, and problem-solving. We aim to create a community where students can explore, experiment, collaborate, and innovate—turning ideas into possibilities and possibilities into impact. Innovate. Automate. Inspire.

Department of Electronics & Instrumentation Engineering

About EIE Department

The Department of Electronics & Instrumentation Engineering (EIE) at Institute of Engineering & Technology (IET), Bundelkhand University, Jhansi is dedicated to nurturing innovative engineers. The department bridges the gap between hardware electronics, smart sensor technologies, process control, robotics, and industrial automation.
