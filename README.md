## 📌 About the Club

The **Innovation & Automation Club (IAC)** serves as a technical hub for students passionate about hardware design, smart control systems, and automation engineering. We bridge the gap between classroom theory and industry application through hands-on learning, prototyping, and faculty mentorship.

### 🎯 Core Focus Areas:
* **Embedded Systems:** Microcontrollers (ESP32, STM32, Arduino), PCB Designing, and Firmware Development.
* **Industrial Automation:** PLC Programming, SCADA Systems, and Process Control.
* **IoT & Smart Sensors:** Cloud Telemetry, Sensor Interfacing, and Wireless Communication.
* **Robotics & Hardware Innovations:** Real-world engineering prototypes and academic research models.
